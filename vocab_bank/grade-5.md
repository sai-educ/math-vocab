# Grade 5 Math Vocabulary


## Operations & Algebraic Thinking (5.OA)

### order of operations

**Definition:** The agreed-upon order for solving a math expression: parentheses first, then multiplication/division, then addition/subtraction.

**Example:** Use the order of operations to solve 3 + 4 x 2.

### parentheses

**Definition:** Symbols ( ) that show which part of an expression to solve first.

**Example:** Solve what's inside the parentheses first.

### expression

**Definition:** A math phrase with numbers and operations, but no equal sign.

**Example:** 3 x (4 + 2) is an expression.

**Common misconception:** Kids sometimes confuse an expression (no equal sign) with an equation (has an equal sign).

### evaluate

**Definition:** Find the value of an expression by solving it.

**Example:** Evaluate the expression 5 + 3 x 2.

### numerical pattern

**Definition:** A sequence of numbers that follows a rule.

**Example:** The numerical pattern adds 5 each time: 5, 10, 15, 20.

### coordinate pair

**Definition:** Two numbers, written as (x, y), that name a point's location on a grid.

**Example:** The coordinate pair (3, 2) marks a point on the graph.


## Number & Operations in Base Ten (5.NBT)

### exponent

**Definition:** A small number that shows how many times to multiply a number by itself.

**Example:** In 10 to the power of 3, the exponent is 3.

### power of ten

**Definition:** A number like 10, 100, or 1,000 made by multiplying 10 by itself.

**Example:** 1,000 is a power of ten.

### round decimals

**Definition:** Change a decimal to the nearest whole number, tenth, or hundredth.

**Example:** Round 3.47 to the nearest tenth: 3.5.

### multiply decimals

**Definition:** Multiply numbers that include decimal points.

**Example:** Multiply decimals: 2.5 x 3 = 7.5.

### divide decimals

**Definition:** Divide numbers that include decimal points.

**Example:** Divide decimals: 6.4 divided by 2 = 3.2.


## Number & Operations—Fractions (5.NF)

### common denominator

**Definition:** A shared bottom number used to add, subtract, or compare fractions.

**Example:** Rewrite the fractions with a common denominator before adding.

### mixed number

**Definition:** A whole number and a fraction together.

**Example:** 3 1/4 is a mixed number.

### multiply fractions

**Definition:** Find the product of two fractions.

**Example:** Multiply fractions: 1/2 x 2/3 = 2/6.

### divide fractions

**Definition:** Find how many times one fraction fits into another.

**Example:** Divide fractions: 1/2 divided by 1/4 = 2.

### simplify

**Definition:** Rewrite a fraction in its smallest, equivalent form.

**Example:** Simplify 4/8 to 1/2.

### reciprocal

**Definition:** A fraction flipped upside down, used to divide fractions.

**Example:** The reciprocal of 2/3 is 3/2.


## Measurement & Data (5.MD)

### volume

**Definition:** The amount of space a solid shape takes up, measured in cubic units.

**Example:** Find the volume of the box.

### cubic unit

**Definition:** A unit used to measure volume, like a cubic inch.

**Example:** The volume is 24 cubic units.

### convert units

**Definition:** Change a measurement from one unit to another, like feet to inches.

**Example:** Convert units: 5 feet equals 60 inches.

### coordinate plane

**Definition:** A grid made of a horizontal and vertical number line, used to plot points.

**Example:** Plot the point on the coordinate plane.

### x-axis

**Definition:** The horizontal number line on a coordinate plane.

**Example:** The point moves 3 spaces along the x-axis.

### y-axis

**Definition:** The vertical number line on a coordinate plane.

**Example:** The point moves 2 spaces up the y-axis.


## Geometry (5.G)

### coordinate plane

**Definition:** A grid formed by a horizontal x-axis and vertical y-axis, used to locate points.

**Example:** Plot (4, 3) on the coordinate plane.

### ordered pair

**Definition:** Two numbers (x, y) that give the exact location of a point on a grid.

**Example:** The ordered pair (2, 5) tells you where to plot the point.

### classify

**Definition:** Sort shapes into groups based on shared attributes.

**Example:** Classify the triangles by their angles.

### hierarchy of shapes

**Definition:** A way of organizing shapes to show how categories relate, like all squares are rectangles.

**Example:** The hierarchy of shapes shows that every square is also a rectangle.
