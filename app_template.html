About this resource

An open educational resource for elementary math vocabulary.

A vocabulary bank of math words spanning Kindergarten through Grade 5, with a child-facing definition, a worked example, and the misconception teachers most often see for each term. Every word is cited to the standard it comes from — the US Common Core, the UK National Curriculum, or India's NCERT — and the Curriculum control above the graph lets you choose which of those to show.

  • Usable Math Developed by the Usable Math team at the University of Massachusetts Amherst.
  • Society & AI In collaboration with the Society & AI research group.

Your privacy

This site collects no data at all. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no cookies, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about you or your students is sent anywhere or stored on any server.

The only things kept are on your own device, in this browser: which words you have opened, and whether sound is on. Clearing your browser data erases them.

Open source

Every part of this tool — the word bank, the readability checks and the code — is public, so educators can see exactly how it was built, reuse it, or adapt it for their own district.
